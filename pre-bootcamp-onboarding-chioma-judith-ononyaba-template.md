## Key Concepts I Learned

- Microsoft Entra ID is Microsoft's cloud-based Identity and Access Management (IAM) solution.
- IAM focuses on authentication (who you are) and authorization (what you can access).
- Entra ID helps organizations securely manage users, applications, and resources.
- Identity is a critical part of cloud security.

---

## Identity Types I Learned

### Human Identities
Used by employees, administrators, contractors, and partners.

### Work or School Accounts
Accounts created and managed within an organization's Entra ID tenant.

### Guest Users
External users invited to collaborate with an organization.

### Service Principals
Identities used by applications and services to access resources securely.

### Managed Identities
Azure-managed identities that eliminate the need to store credentials in code.

---

## Benefits of Identity Management

- Improved security
- Better access control
- Easier user administration
- Regulatory compliance
- Reduced risk of unauthorized access
