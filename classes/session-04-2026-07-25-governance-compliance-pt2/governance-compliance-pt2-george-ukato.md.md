## My Takeaways

<!-- What was most valuable to you personally from this session? -->



* Soft Delete Retention Configuration: From 14 to 180days (Default- 14days)
* Azure RBAC roles deals with Azure resources while Microsoft Entra roles deals with directory (Tenant)
* Azure has about 1000 built-in roles and supports 4000 role assignment per subscription.
* Assign RBAC roles to groups instead of users.
* Once you enable CMK on a vault you cannot revert to the default Platform Managed Keys.
* CMK works only on a new Vault with no back-up items registered yet; You can't migrate an existing vault.
